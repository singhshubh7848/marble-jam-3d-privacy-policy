Privacy Policy

Game: Marble Jam 3D: Color Sort Package: com.marblejam.colorsort Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Welcome to Marble Jam 3D: Color Sort ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your use of our mobile game application. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, storage, and protection of information when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect and Store

Marble Jam 3D is designed with a privacy-first approach. We minimize data collection and prioritize local, on-device operations:

A. Local Gameplay Data (Stored On-Device Only)

All core gameplay progression and settings are saved strictly in your device's local storage via standard browser storage APIs and SQLite. This includes:

  • Level progression, star ratings, and win streaks
  • In-game virtual coin balances
  • Power-up & booster inventory (Smart Shuffle, Power Hammer, Rainbow Paint)
  • Customization preferences (equipped avatars and 3D environment themes)
  • Audio, vibration, and language preferences

Note: We do not sync, store, or sell this gameplay progress to external private servers without your explicit consent.

B. Technical & Device Information

When you play the game, non-personally identifiable technical information may be processed by integrated third-party SDKs for performance, crash diagnostics, and advertisement delivery:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version (e.g., Android version)
  • Screen resolution, graphics hardware capabilities, and language settings
  • Coarse diagnostic data (e.g., anonymous crash logs and frame-rate stability metrics)
  • Advertising Identifier (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID), subject to your device privacy controls and consent settings

2. Third-Party Services and Partners

To provide optional rewarded video advertisements, in-app purchases, and native mobile functionality, Marble Jam 3D integrates trusted third-party SDKs that operate in compliance with applicable data protection laws:

• Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to deliver opt-in rewarded video advertisements (e.g., earning extra coins or power-ups) and non-intrusive interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Learn more: Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

• Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC)

All in-app purchases (such as coin bundles, theme passes, and booster packs) are processed directly and securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We never receive, store, or process your credit card numbers, bank information, or billing credentials.

• Capacitor Framework & Device Features

The game utilizes Capacitor open-source plugins strictly for on-device features:

  • Haptics: Provides tactile vibration feedback during gameplay interactions.
  • Local Notifications: Sends scheduled on-device reminders (e.g., daily challenges or level milestones). No remote push notification server tracks your device.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Marble Jam 3D is a casual puzzle game created for players of all ages. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Advertisements served within the game adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines and family-friendly ad categories.
  • If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps to remove it.

4. User Rights & Data Management (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Reset / Delete Data: Because game progress is stored locally on your device, you can completely erase all data at any time by clearing the game's cache and data in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Marble Jam 3D > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising ID Opt-Out: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertisements anytime in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
  • Data Access / Inquiry: You can contact us with any questions regarding data handling and privacy rights.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your data. All communication with third-party partners (such as Google Play and AdMob) occurs over encrypted, secure HTTPS connections.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates, new features, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage players to review this page periodically.
